Troubleshooting

When something is not right.

Why my play mat doesn't fitThe mat is meant to be a snug fit. If it reads as too long, adjust the rails to give the vault a little more room, then massage the slack out — never trim it.My table wobbles or feels unevenAlmost always hardware that isn't fully tightened — and the one people miss is the bolt that runs down through the table surface into the leg.My assembly hardware never arrivedCheck the leg and frame cartons first — hardware ships packed with them, not loose. If it genuinely isn't there, we send a replacement set free, no return.
