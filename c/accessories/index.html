Other accessories & fit help

Connecting two Pangea tablesRails lift off any edge, so two Pangeas join into one surface — two 3×5s make a 3×10.5 or a 6.5×5.Where do the toppers go?They have to live somewhere when the vault is open — the padded Storage Cube is the purpose-built answer, and the XL also takes leaves and dividers.Which accessories fit my tableRail accessories clip to the Omnitrack rail and fit both the Pangea and Tavern; toppers, panels, and play mats are made per table size and are not interchangeable.Why my play mat doesn't fitThe mat is meant to be a snug fit. If it reads as too long, adjust the rails to give the vault a little more room, then massage the slack out — never trim it.Will this topper or accessory fit my table?Toppers, panels, and play mats are made per table size (3x3, 3x5, 4x6) and are not interchangeable — but cup holders, Flex trays, and Flex inserts snap into the Omnitrack rail and work across the table line.
