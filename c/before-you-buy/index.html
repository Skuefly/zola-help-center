Before you buy

Choosing a table, sizing it to your room, and what things cost.

Accessory bundles explainedPlayer-count bundles are the standing sets; Signature Bundles are 35% off but only with a table. The player number means accessories, not seating.Choosing your leg heightStandard (26" clearance) suits dining chairs and is the default; Counter (32") needs stools; Coffee (14") is for lounge seating.Discounts, bundles, and how to saveWe don't run discount codes, but there are two real ways to save — Signature Bundles at 35% off with a table purchase, and 15% off Pangea dining chairs with a Pangea Table purchase.Is it in stock, and when do you restock?Many of our tables are on pre-order right now — you can still order, and the estimated ship date is shown on that product's own page, which we keep updated as inventory arrives.Pangea dining chairsThe Pangea Mid-Century Dining Chairs are sold in sets of two, come in two colors, and are 15% off with a Pangea Table purchase — the only discount we offer outside of Signature Bundles.Pangea or Tavern — which one?Pangea is the recessed-vault gaming table for a home; Tavern is the height-adjustable one built for cafés and flexible spaces.Paying over timeShop Pay Installments is available at checkout on US orders — interest-free split payments under $1,000, and 3, 6, or 12 monthly payments with interest above it. Every full table sits in the second group.Seeing a table in personCheck the Dealer Locator for authorized dealers near you, then contact the dealer directly to ask what they have in stock and on display. There's no Zola showroom, and we don't offer local pickup.Signature BundlesSignature Bundles are our biggest saving — 35% off a curated accessory collection, available only alongside a table purchase, matched to your table size and player count.Tables for a game café, venue, or rentalCommercial use is covered — one year across structure, rails, and lift systems, with free hardware any time. Get in touch through our contact page at https://zolamod.com/pages/contact-us and our B2B contact will scope configuration, durability, and ongoing parts support with you.Wholesale and volume pricingRetailers who want to stock Zola apply through the Retail Partners form. Cafés, venues, and anyone buying multiple tables for their own space go through the contact page, and our B2B contact replies within one business day.Will it fit my room?Measure against the overall footprint, not the playable size, and leave about 3 feet clear on every side you'll seat players.Will my games fit?Measure your biggest game's board plus player boards against the playable area — 36×36, 36×60, or 48×72 — rather than sizing by player count.
