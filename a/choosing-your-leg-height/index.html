Choosing your leg height

Standard (26" clearance) suits dining chairs and is the default; Counter (32") needs stools; Coffee (14") is for lounge seating.

Three heights are available, and the option name refers to the clearance underneath rather than the overall table height.

OptionClearance underneathOverall heightPairs with
Standard26″30.5″Dining chairs — the default
Counter32″36.5″Counter stools
Coffee14″18.5″Sofa and lounge seating

The tabletop adds 4.5″ over the clearance figure in every configuration, so overall height is always clearance + 4.5″.

Standard is what most people want. At 30.5″ overall that's normal dining-table height, and it's what our Pangea Mid-Century Dining Chairs are made for.

Counter legs add $100.

Choose seating and height together

This is the one that trips people up. Our dining chairs suit the Standard height only. We don't sell counter stools or lounge seating, so if you choose Counter or Coffee you're sourcing seating elsewhere — and that's much easier to plan before the table arrives than after.

  • Counter (32″) — 36.5″ overall, so you'll want stools with roughly a 24–26″ seat height. Standing players are comfortable at this height too, which is part of the appeal for a games room.
  • Coffee (14″) — 18.5″ overall, designed for playing from a sofa or floor cushions. It's a genuinely different way to use the table, and not one that works for long sessions with a big game.

Changing height later

Legs are sold separately — $150 for Standard and Coffee, $250 for Counter — so a height change later is a leg swap rather than a new table.

Before ordering a replacement set, message us through our contact page with your table size and what you're trying to end up with. Leg sets are handled case by case and it's worth confirming the fit for your configuration first.
