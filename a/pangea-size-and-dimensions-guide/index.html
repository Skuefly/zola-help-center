Pangea size and dimensions guide

Pangea comes in 3x3, 3x5, and 4x6 playable sizes with Standard 26-inch, Coffee 14-inch, or Counter 32-inch legs in Dark Walnut or Cerused Oak — and the overall footprint runs about 3.25 inches (8.3 cm) larger per side than the playable area.

Everything you need to pick a size, a leg height, and a finish in one place. If you only read one section, read the footprint table — the size in the product name is the playable area, not the outside of the table.

The difference between the two columns is the Omnitrack magnetic rail that runs around the perimeter — roughly 3.25 inches (8.3 cm) per side. Measure your room against the overall footprint, and plan your game footprints against the playable area.

Leg heights

  • Standard — 26″ (66 cm) — pairs with typical dining chairs; the default for most setups
  • Coffee — 14″ (35.5 cm) — lounge and sofa seating, living-room installs
  • Counter — 32″ (81 cm) — stools, standing play, and setups where you want to see the whole board without leaning in

Finishes

Dark Walnut and Cerused Oak. Cerused Oak has visible open grain with lighter lines worked into it — that texture is intentional. Table Topper Sets and panels come in the same two finishes so later additions match.

How many people each one seats

  • Pangea 3×5 — seats 6.
  • Pangea 4×6 — comfortably seats 8 to 10 players.
  • Pangea 3×3 — built for cozier spaces and compact tabletop sessions, with a more intimate footprint. Its product page is the place to look for how it sits in a room.

One thing worth clearing up: bundle names like "(6 Players)" or "(8 Players)" describe how many players' worth of accessories are included. They aren't the table's seating capacity.

Choosing a size

  • 3×3 — smaller apartments and rooms, two-player and duel-scale games, or as a coffee-height centerpiece.
  • 3×5 — the middle ground. Comfortable for a regular group without demanding a dedicated room.
  • 4×6 — the sprawl size. Big-box games with player boards, trays, and a shared map, with room for everyone's drink on the rails.

If you have a specific game in mind and want to know whether its footprint works on a given size, reach out through our contact page — we're happy to talk through it rather than have you guess.

Modularity

Pangea is modular. A 3×3 base can be extended toward larger configurations using table dividers (which include an Omnitrack rail) plus additional panels and frames. Configurations vary, so confirm the specifics with us before ordering parts for a particular layout — get in touch through our contact page with what you have and what you're trying to build.
