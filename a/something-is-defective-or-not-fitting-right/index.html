Something is defective or not fitting right

Send a photo and your order number through our contact page at https://zolamod.com/pages/contact-us with a short description of what's off — a magnet or panel not sitting flush, a leg or hardware issue, a finish concern — and our team will follow up with the fix.

Let's get it sorted. Send a photo through our contact page with your order number and a short description of what's off, and our team will follow up with the fix — a replacement part, corrected hardware, or guidance on the assembly step.

What to include

  • Your order number
  • A photo of the issue — a wide shot plus a close-up if you can
  • A short description in plain language

Photos let us get you the right solution the first time rather than trading messages while your table sits half-assembled.

Things worth telling us about

  • A magnet or panel that isn't sitting flush
  • A leg that doesn't seat, or hardware that won't line up
  • A table that rocks or feels uneven once assembled
  • A finish that looks wrong to you

None of these are things you should live with. Reach out.

Before you get in touch — two quick checks

If you're mid-assembly, it's worth confirming the step. Components are precision-fit, and pieces sometimes need to go together in a specific order. See Where are my assembly instructions? — and if you're stuck on a specific step, tell us which one and we'll walk you through it.

If it's the Cerused Oak finish you're looking at, the visible open grain and lighter lines are intentional — that texture is part of the finish rather than a flaw. See Caring for your Zola finish. If it still looks wrong to you after reading that, send a photo and we'll take a look.

Which path is this?

  • Arrived damaged from freight → My order arrived damaged
  • A part is missing or broken in the box → Missing or broken replacement parts
  • Failed in normal use, inside its coverage period → Zola warranty coverage

If you're not sure, reach out anyway. Sorting the paperwork is our job.
