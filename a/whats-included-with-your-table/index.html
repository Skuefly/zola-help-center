What's included with your table

Frame, legs, topper set, panels, a play mat sized for your vault, and the assembly hardware — accessories are separate.

In the box

  • The frame — powder-coated steel
  • Legs, in the height you selected
  • The Table Topper Set — the panels that convert the vault to a flat surface
  • Base panels
  • A play mat, sized for your vault
  • Assembly hardware

That's a complete, usable table. Nothing on the list below is required to play on it.

What's separate

Everything that clips to the rail or sits on the surface is an accessory bought separately: cup holders, Flex trays and inserts, card and chit organizers, armrests, player desks and shelves, tablet holders, storage bins, table dividers, and the Topper Storage Cube.

Chairs are separate too, and there's 15% off the Pangea Mid-Century Dining Chairs when you buy them alongside a table.

Two things worth knowing before it arrives

The play mat is included. People often add one to the cart thinking it's needed — it isn't. The standalone mat listings are for replacements. See Play mats explained.

Topper storage isn't included. The topper panels have to live somewhere when the vault is open, and the Storage Cube is the purpose-built answer at $129.99 (or $199.99 for the XL, which also takes leaves and dividers). It isn't required, but it's the accessory people most often wish they'd added to the original order — see Where do the toppers go?.

Ordering accessories with the table

Worth doing if you already know what you want. Accessories ship parcel and the table ships freight, so they arrive separately either way — but a single order avoids paying shipping twice, and Signature Bundles are 35% off but only available alongside a table purchase. See Signature Bundles.

Assembly

The table is customer-assembled. Instructions are sent per model rather than printed in the carton — see Where are my assembly instructions? and How long assembly takes and what you need.
