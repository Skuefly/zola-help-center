Signature Bundles

Signature Bundles are our biggest saving — 35% off a curated accessory collection, available only alongside a table purchase, matched to your table size and player count.

The full Pangea experience. A thoughtfully curated collection of accessories, selected for immersive play — at 35% off. It's the biggest saving we offer.

The one catch: it comes with a table

Signature Bundles are an exclusive offer with a table purchase. They're added to a table order rather than sold on their own, so they aren't something you'll come across browsing the accessory catalog. If you're buying a Pangea, this is the moment to decide — it's not an offer you can come back for later on its own.

What the pieces do

  • Table Dividers — split or section the surface, and each pair includes an Omnitrack rail
  • Flex Trays — the foundation of the Flex system. They mount magnetically to the rail without tools or clamps, hold the Flex inserts, and clear the main play surface
  • Armrests — for the sessions that run long
  • Cup Holders — drinks off the play surface and outside the vault, with a removable insert
  • Game Piece Organizers — universal chit holders
  • Player Shelves — personal space for each player's cards, tokens, and reference sheets
  • Topper Storage Cube XL — padded storage sized for both the topper panels and the dividers, so the finished panels aren't stacked against a wall collecting scuffs
  • Storage Bins — stackable gear bins

Is it the right call?

If you're outfitting the table properly anyway, it is — the saving is real and it arrives in one go, matched to your finish, rather than assembled over months of separate orders.

If you play mostly two-player, or you already own a good chunk of the accessory range, the standalone accessory bundles may fit better.

Not sure which way to go? Tell us your table size, your usual player count, and what you already own through our contact page, and we'll give you a straight answer.
