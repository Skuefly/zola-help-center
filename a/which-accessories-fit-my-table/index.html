Which accessories fit my table

Rail accessories clip to the Omnitrack rail and fit both the Pangea and Tavern; toppers, panels, and play mats are made per table size and are not interchangeable.

The short version: if it snaps into the Omnitrack rail, it fits both the Pangea and the Tavern — they share the same magnetic rail. If it covers or lines the play surface, it's made to match your Pangea's size and won't fit a different one. The one exception on the rail is the Player Desk, which fits the Pangea only.

Three accessory lines, and they all coexist

None of these replaced another — pick based on where the table lives.

  • Pangea originals — solid oak. The original line, for a home setup where you want the accessories to match the furniture.
  • Utility Series — fiberglass-reinforced nylon. Built for durable, high-traffic use: board game cafés and tabletop gaming retail stores. Only two products come in a Utility format — the Flex Tray and the Cup Holder.
  • Flex Accessories — the inserts. Card holders, the deck chit holder, and the bins. They fit the recessed tray area of the Flex Tray (either the oak or the Utility version) and also work standalone on the play surface.

Running a café, a store, or anywhere the table gets handled all day? Go Utility. Outfitting a home table? Oak.

Size-specific — made per Pangea size, order to match
