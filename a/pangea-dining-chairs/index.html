Pangea dining chairs

The Pangea Mid-Century Dining Chairs are sold in sets of two, come in two colors, and are 15% off with a Pangea Table purchase — the only discount we offer outside of Signature Bundles.

Pangea Mid-Century Dining Chairs are expertly engineered to provide superior design and comfort to every table.

What they are

The mid-century modern-inspired frame features tapered back legs and an oval-shaped backrest in a richly grained finish. Constructed with durability and comfort in mind, the elegant design is complete with an upholstered, cushioned seat.

  • Sold in sets of two
  • Available in two colors

15% off with a Pangea Table

Buy a Pangea Table and the chairs come 15% off. It's the only discount we offer outside of Signature Bundles, so it's worth adding the chairs to the same order rather than coming back for them.

How many do I need?

That depends on the table rather than the chairs, and chairs are sold two at a time:

  • Pangea 3×3 — seats 4, so two sets
  • Pangea 3×5 — seats 6, so three sets
  • Pangea 4×6 — seats 8 to 10, so four or five sets

Leg height matters as much as chair count. These chairs are sized for the Standard 26″ legs. If you've gone Counter 32″ you'll want stool seating instead, and Coffee 14″ pairs with sofa or floor seating rather than dining chairs. See Choosing your leg height, and Chair specifications and weight capacity for dimensions and the 300 lb rating.
