Commercial use is covered

One year across frame, legs, welds, joinery, load-bearing hardware, rails, accessory hardware, and both crank and electric lift systems. Hardware and fasteners are free any time, in a commercial space exactly as at home.

Worth stating plainly, because it used to be the opposite: commercial use no longer voids the warranty. If you were quoted or told otherwise before, the current terms are what apply — see Zola warranty coverage for the full component table.

What counts as commercial is use within your trade, business, craft, or profession, or in any space open to the public or to members. Cafés, bars, restaurants, game and hobby stores, libraries, schools, event and convention venues, clubs, coworking and common areas.

The shorter commercial period isn't a technicality. A table doing forty hours a week of open play has a genuinely different wear profile from the same table doing one game night a week, and a single number across both would be dishonest in one direction or the other.

What's still on you

Two things a commercial buyer should plan around rather than discover:

  • Wear to the play surface, toppers, foam, and finish isn't covered in any setting. In a busy room those are consumables — they wear, and replacements are purchasable. Budget for them the way you'd budget for anything else in a high-traffic space. A defect in those same parts is covered.
  • Sanitising products matter. Damage from cleaning agents or disinfectants outside our care guide isn't covered, and a room getting wiped down several times a day is much harder on a finish than a home. Ask us before you standardise on a product. See Caring for your Zola finish.
