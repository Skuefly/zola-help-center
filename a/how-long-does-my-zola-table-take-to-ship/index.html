How long does my Zola table take to ship?

It depends on whether that item is in stock or on pre-order — the estimated ship date is listed right on the product's own page, and once it ships it's 7-10 business days in transit plus a call from the freight carrier to schedule.

There's an honest answer and a made-up one, and we'd rather give you the honest one: it depends on whether that specific model and finish is in stock or on pre-order right now. That's why the single most accurate date lives on the product page itself, not in an article.

The date on the product page is the one to trust

Most of our tables are on pre-order at the moment. When that's the case, the estimated ship date is listed directly on that product's page, and we keep it current as inventory lands. It reflects the real production and arrival schedule for that exact model, so it will always be more accurate than a general number quoted from memory — by us, by our chat assistant, or by an article like this one.

Open the page for the model, size, and finish you want, and look for the estimated ship date. If you're not sure which listing is yours, reach out through our contact page with the model and finish and we'll point you to it.

If the item is in stock

  • Normal conditions: about one week to process before it leaves the warehouse.
  • Right after a restock: closer to three weeks. When new inventory lands, we work through the backorder queue first, in order, before we're back to the one-week norm. This is our busy season reality, and we'd rather tell you upfront than have you wondering on day ten.

Then, always: transit and the carrier call

Once your table leaves the warehouse, expect 7-10 business days in transit. You'll get a confirmation email with tracking, and the freight carrier (typically ABF) will reach out to arrange a delivery appointment — tables ship LTL freight, not by parcel, so someone needs to be there to receive it.

Targeting a specific date?

If you're aiming at a birthday, a holiday, a move-in, or a game night you've already announced, tell us the date when you order — or get in touch through our contact page before you order. We'll look at the current schedule for that exact item and tell you straight whether it's realistic. We'd much rather talk you into a different configuration that lands in time than have you waiting on the one that doesn't.
