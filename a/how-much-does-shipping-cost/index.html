How much does shipping cost?

Shipping is calculated at checkout based on your address and what's in the cart — tables move as oversize freight, so the cost reflects a truck delivery rather than a parcel rate.

Shipping is calculated and shown at checkout, based on your delivery address and what's in your cart. Add the items you want, enter your address, and you'll see the real number before you pay — we'd rather show it plainly than bury it in the product price.

Why table shipping costs what it does

Zola tables are built from commercial-grade, high-density engineered wood. They're solid and heavy, which is exactly why they feel the way they do — and also why they move by LTL freight on a truck rather than by UPS or FedEx. Freight on oversize furniture is real money. We're upfront about that instead of padding the sticker price to make shipping look free.

Accessories ship differently

Flex inserts, cup holders, trays, card holders, play mats, and other smaller accessories don't need freight. Their shipping is calculated at checkout the same way, but at normal parcel rates.

Ways to get more value out of one shipment

  • Order accessories with your table. Consolidating what you want into one order is generally more efficient than a separate order later.
  • Look at the bundles. Signature Bundles are 35% off the pieces bought separately, and buying Pangea Mid-Century Dining Chairs alongside a table takes 15% off the chairs. Those are the two discounts we run — there are no codes. See Discount codes and bundles.

Where we ship

Within the United States for now. See Do you ship internationally? for where that stands.
