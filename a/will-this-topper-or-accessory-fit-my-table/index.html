Will this topper or accessory fit my table?

Toppers, panels, and play mats are made per table size (3x3, 3x5, 4x6) and are not interchangeable — but cup holders, Flex trays, and Flex inserts snap into the Omnitrack rail and work across the table line.

There are two answers, depending on which kind of item you're asking about.

Size-specific: toppers, panels, and mats

Table Topper Sets, Table Panels, and play mats are made per table size — 3×3, 3×5, and 4×6 — and they are not interchangeable. A 3×3 topper will not fit a 4×6. These pieces are precision-fit to the recessed vault, which is exactly what makes a topper sit flat and flush, so order the one matching your table's size.

When you order, match:

  • Size — 3×3, 3×5, or 4×6
  • Finish — Dark Walnut or Cerused Oak, so a later addition matches the table you already own

Universal: rail accessories

Cup holders, Flex trays, and Flex inserts snap into the Omnitrack rail and work across the table line, including the Tavern Sit-Stand tables, which include the magnetic rail system. You don't order these by table size — a Flex Tray is a Flex Tray.

The Flex inserts (card holders, deck and chit holders, divided bins, tall bins) seat into the Flex Tray, so if you're building out a player station, the tray is the piece that connects it to the table.

Play mats — one note

The Zola Tristar Grid Play Mat is offered in 3×3 and 3×5. A 4×6 mat is not sold as a standard listing — reach out through our contact page if you want one for a 4×6 and we'll tell you what's possible.

Not sure what you have?

Tell us the exact model, size, finish, and leg height — or send your order number — through our contact page, and we'll confirm the right part before you buy. That's faster than a return, and returns on furniture are no fun for anyone.
