Discounts, bundles, and how to save

We don't run discount codes, but there are two real ways to save — Signature Bundles at 35% off with a table purchase, and 15% off Pangea dining chairs with a Pangea Table purchase.

We don't run discount codes. Not for holidays, not for newsletters, not for first orders. What we do run is two straightforward offers, and between them they're worth considerably more than a code would be.

1. Signature Bundles — save 35%

The biggest saving on the store. A thoughtfully curated collection of accessories, selected for the full Pangea experience and immersive play, at 35% off.

It's an exclusive offer with a table purchase — Signature Bundles are added to a table order rather than bought on their own, so you won't find them browsing the catalog on their own.

2. 15% off chairs with a Pangea Table

Buy a Pangea Table and the Pangea Mid-Century Dining Chairs are 15% off. They're sold in sets of two, in two colors.

This is the only discount we offer outside of Signature Bundles. More on the chairs: Pangea dining chairs.

Why no codes

A code system means the price you see depends on whether you happened to find the right forum post that week. We'd rather price the product honestly and put the savings somewhere everyone can find them.

Military, first responder, repeat customer

We appreciate the ask, genuinely, and the answer is the same for everyone: no codes. The Signature Bundles and the chair offer are the best pricing we have.

Picking the right one

The bundle that saves you the most is the one matching how many people actually sit at your table on a regular game night. Two regulars and occasional guests is a different build than a standing group of six.

Worth knowing: a bundle named "(6 Players)" describes how many players' worth of accessories it contains, not how many people the table seats.

Tell us your table size and how many players you're outfitting through our contact page and we'll point you to the one that fits. Or see Which accessories fit my table?.
