Seeing a table in person

Check the Dealer Locator for authorized dealers near you, then contact the dealer directly to ask what they have in stock and on display. There's no Zola showroom, and we don't offer local pickup.

Start with the Dealer Locator. It lists the authorized dealers carrying Zola tables, so it's the fastest way to find out whether there's one within reach of you.

Then contact the dealer directly. What they have in stock and what's actually set up on their floor are two different things, both change, and the dealer is the only one who knows. A quick call before you drive is worth it — ask specifically whether the model and finish you're interested in is on display, not just whether they carry the line.

There's no Zola showroom of our own.

We don't offer local pickup

Every order placed with us ships. There's no warehouse pickup or will-call, so freight is part of buying direct.

If a dealer near you stocks what you want, buying from them is the way to skip freight entirely — worth checking before you order, since freight on a table is a real line item.

If nothing's within reach

Most of what people want from an in-person look is answerable remotely, and usually more precisely:

  • Scale. Mask out the overall footprint on your floor with tape — 42.5″ × 66.5″ for a 3×5, 54.5″ × 78.5″ for a 4×6 — and live with it for a day. That tells you more about whether it fits your room than standing next to one in a shop.
  • Whether your games fit. Measure your biggest game's actual spread against the playable area. See Will my games fit?.
  • Finish. Dark Walnut and Cerused Oak read differently in different light, and Cerused Oak's open grain with lighter lines worked into it is deliberate — worth knowing before it arrives, so it reads as the design rather than a surprise. See Caring for your Zola finish.
  • Height and seating. Standard legs give 26″ clearance, 30.5″ overall — the same as a normal dining table. Sit at the one you have now.

Ask us the awkward questions instead

If there's something specific you'd want to check by touching one — how solid the standing deck feels, how the toppers seat, how heavy a panel is to lift — just ask through our contact page. We'll answer plainly, including when the honest answer isn't flattering.
