Cup holders and drink accessories

Cup holders clip to the Omnitrack rail and take mug and wine-glass adapters — the simplest way to keep drinks off the playing surface entirely.

A drink on the playing surface is the most likely thing to damage a game or a table. Cup holders clip into the Omnitrack rail around the vault, which moves the glass off the surface entirely rather than just giving it a coaster.

The adapters drop into the cup holder rather than replacing it, so one rail position handles a tumbler, a mug, or a stemmed glass depending on what's seated in it. The bundle is the cheaper route if you want both.

Where they go

Anywhere along the Omnitrack rail. Because they clamp rather than mount to a fixed point, you can position them per player and move them between sessions — including onto the outer edges when two tables are joined.

How many

Practically, one per player is the norm, which is why they're sold in pairs. Rail length is the only real limit, and on any table size you'll run out of players before you run out of rail.

Standard or Utility

The standard cup holder is solid oak; the Utility Series version is the hard-wearing line for cafés and high-traffic use. Same job, different material — see The Utility Series and the standard line.

For a home table, the oak one. For a venue where dozens of strangers handle it weekly, the Utility.

Fits both Pangea and Tavern

Cup holders clip to the magnetic rail, and both the Pangea and the Tavern use the same magnetic rail — so they fit either table. See Which accessories fit my table?, and send a photo through our contact page if you have any questions about your setup.
