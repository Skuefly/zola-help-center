Where are my assembly instructions?

Reach out through our contact page at zolamod.com/pages/contact-us with your order number and we'll send the assembly instructions for your exact model — and if you're mid-assembly and stuck on a step, tell us which one.

Happy to get those to you. Reach out through our contact page with your order number and we'll send the assembly instructions for your exact model.

Why we send them per model

Pangea is modular, and what's in your boxes depends on your size, leg height, and which components you ordered. Sending the instructions matched to your actual order is more useful than pointing you at a generic document that covers four configurations you don't have.

If you're stuck mid-assembly

Tell us which step you're on and what you're seeing. That's usually enough for us to sort it out in one reply — send it through our contact page, and a photo of the step in question helps more than anything else you could send.

Common places people get stuck:

  • Component order — precision-fit pieces sometimes need to go together in a specific sequence
  • Hardware that seems to be missing (check Where is the assembly hardware? — it's often packed with the legs or frame hardware)
  • A panel or leg that won't seat, which may be an assembly-order issue or may be a part issue — see Something is defective or not fitting right

Before you start

  • Give yourself floor space around the table footprint, not only the table's own footprint
  • These are heavy pieces. A second set of hands makes the whole thing faster and safer
  • Open every box and lay out the hardware before you begin, so a missing piece turns up at minute five rather than minute fifty
