Do you ship internationally?

Not yet — everything ships within the US for now, tables and accessories alike, and we're aiming to open up international shipping in late 2026.

Not yet. Everything ships within the United States for now — tables and accessories alike.

Why not yet

Tables move by freight, and international freight on a piece of furniture this size runs into the thousands. That isn't a cost we'd feel good handing to a customer, and shipping something premium halfway around the world on a route we haven't proven is how tables arrive damaged. We'd rather do it properly than do it early.

What's coming

We're aiming to open up international shipping in late 2026. We're not going to promise a date, a country list, or a rate until we can actually stand behind them — but it's an active project, not a someday.

Get on the list

If you'd like to hear from us when international shipping goes live, get in touch through our contact page and we'll keep you on the list. Tell us your country and which model you have your eye on; that helps us prioritize where we open first.

If you see something that says otherwise

Our published shipping policy page is being updated. If you find a page or an automated reply suggesting international shipping is available, this article is the current answer — US only for now. Reach out through our contact page if you'd like it confirmed directly.
