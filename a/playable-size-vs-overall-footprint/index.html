Playable size vs. overall footprint

The size in the product name is the playable area (the recessed vault) — the overall footprint is about 3.25 inches (8.3 cm) larger per side because of the Omnitrack rails, so a 4x6 measures roughly 54.5 x 78.5 inches (138 x 199 cm) overall.

This is the single most common point of confusion, and it's worth two minutes before you measure your room.

Which number to measure your room against

Use the overall footprint. Then add clearance for chairs and for people walking behind them. A table that technically fits wall-to-wall is a table nobody can sit at comfortably.

Which number matters for game footprints

Use the playable area. When you're checking whether a specific game's board, player boards, and card rows fit, the recessed vault is the surface you're working with.

Height

Leg heights are Standard 26″ (66 cm), Coffee 14″ (35.5 cm), and Counter 32″ (81 cm). Choose based on the seating you already own or plan to buy — the standard height pairs with typical dining chairs, coffee height with lounge seating, and counter height with stools.
