Accessory bundles explained

Player-count bundles are the standing sets; Signature Bundles are 35% off but only with a table. The player number means accessories, not seating.

Two different things get called bundles, and they work differently.

Player-count accessory bundles

Standing products you can buy any time, sized by how many players you're kitting out:

BundlePrice
Pangea Accessory Bundle (2 Players)$109.99
Pangea Accessory Bundle (4 Players)$169.99
Pangea Accessory Bundle (6 Players)$269.99

There are also focused ones — the Drink Adapter Bundle ($55.98) pairs the mug and wine-glass adapters, and the Player Desk Bundle (4 Players) ($509.95) is four desks plus the 4-up stand.

There are also Utility Series Accessory Bundles — the hard-wearing café-grade line — in 2- and 4-player versions.

Signature Bundles — 35% off, table purchase only

These are the curated sets at a real discount, and they're only available alongside a table purchase. They appear as an option while you're buying a table rather than as products you can find later.

That's worth knowing before you check out: if you dismiss the option and decide later you wanted it, the 35% isn't available on its own. See Signature Bundles.

The player number means accessories, not seating

"(6 Players)" describes how many players' worth of accessories are in the box. It is not a statement about how many people the table seats.

A 4-player bundle on a 4×6 that seats 8–10 is a perfectly normal purchase — you're kitting out four positions, not four seats. Seating comes from the table: 3×3 seats 4, 3×5 seats 6, 4×6 seats 8–10.

Working out whether a bundle saves you money

Bundles are priced below the same items bought individually, but the right question is whether you'd buy all of them. A bundle containing two things you want and three you don't isn't a saving.

The prices in Which accessories fit my table? let you add up the pieces you'd actually choose and compare. If it's close, buy the individual items — you end up with exactly what you use.

Material, not table

The Pangea and Utility Series bundles differ by material, not by which table they fit. Both attach to the same magnetic rail, and the Pangea and Tavern share that rail — so either bundle works on either table. The Pangea bundles are the solid-oak line; the Utility Series bundles are the hard-wearing café-grade line. Choose by durability and look. See Which accessories fit my table?.
