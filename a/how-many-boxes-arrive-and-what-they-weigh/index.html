How many boxes arrive, and what they weigh

About three cartons per table, 133–242 lbs total depending on size, with the longest carton running 47" to 83".

A Pangea ships as roughly three cartons — frame, topper set, and panels — rather than one crate. Accessories ship separately by parcel.

Weights

TableAssembled weight
Pangea 3×3133 lbs / 60 kg
Pangea 3×5182 lbs / 83 kg
Pangea 4×6242 lbs / 110 kg
Tavern 44×3065 lbs / 29 kg
Tavern 44×6097 lbs / 44 kg

Those are assembled weights. No single carton is that heavy — the load is split across the three — but it's the number that matters for whether you can move the finished table later. (Short answer: not alone, and not through a standard doorway.)

The number that decides whether it gets inside

The longest carton is what gets stuck, not the heaviest one:

TableLongest carton
3×3about 47″
3×5about 71″
4×6about 83″

Measure the narrowest point on the route in — the doorway, the turn at the top of the stairs, the elevator's diagonal. A 4×6's 83″ carton is nearly seven feet and doesn't pivot in a tight stairwell.

If the route is marginal, tell us before the order ships and we'll talk it through.

Boxes may not all arrive together

Freight cartons for one table normally travel as a single shipment, but accessories ship parcel and arrive separately — often before or after the table, from a different carrier.

If your table arrives and an accessory hasn't, that's expected. If a table carton is missing from the freight delivery, note it on the paperwork before the driver leaves and contact us the same day — see My order arrived damaged, which covers short shipments as well as damage.

Don't move the cartons upstairs unopened if you can avoid it

The delivery is curbside, so getting the cartons inside is on you. It's usually easier to move components than sealed boxes — but don't unbox on the driveway. Open them where you'll build, so you're not carrying loose panels through the house.
