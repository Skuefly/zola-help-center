Where we ship

Tables ship freight to the continental US and Alaska. Parcel items also reach Hawaii, Puerto Rico, and US territories. No international shipping yet.

Tables — continental US and Alaska

Tables move by LTL freight. That covers the continental United States and Alaska.

Accessories — wider

Anything that ships parcel rather than freight — cup holders, trays, mats, organizers, dividers, storage cubes — can also go to Hawaii, Puerto Rico, Guam, and US territories.

So if you already own a table somewhere we can't send freight, you can still buy accessories for it.

Internationally — not yet

We don't ship internationally at this time. We're aiming to open international shipping in late 2026, and it isn't something we can do partially in the meantime — a table is a freight shipment, and the customs and carrier side has to be properly in place rather than improvised per order.

If you want to know when that changes, our newsletter and Instagram are where it'll be announced first. See Do you ship internationally?.

Using a US address

If you're outside the US but have a US shipping address, an order to that address works like any other domestic order — the freight restrictions above still apply, so a table needs an address we can send freight to.

What we can't help with is what happens after that: onward freight forwarding, export paperwork, customs, and duties are yours to arrange, and we can't act as exporter of record or advise on them.

Alaska deliveries take longer than a lower-48 route and the freight cost is higher — it's calculated at checkout like anywhere else, but it's worth looking at the number before you commit.

If you're not sure

Enter your address at checkout — shipping is calculated there, and unavailable destinations will show up. If something looks wrong or errors out, send us a screenshot through our contact page and we'll look at it.
